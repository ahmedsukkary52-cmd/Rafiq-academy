# ADR-008 — Student Achievements Redesign

**Status:** Pending

**Date:** 2026-07-22

---

## Context

During the first architecture investigation of the Student Achievements feature, we discovered that
the current implementation contains overlapping concepts and inconsistent ownership.

The project currently contains multiple representations of the same business concept:

- Student achievements
- Teacher awards
- Supervisor-issued achievements

Although they all use the same Firestore collection (`achievements`), they do not share the same
document schema or business logic.

Examples of inconsistencies:

- Different document fields (`date` vs `grantedAt`)
- Different entity models
- Different repository ownership
- Different business rules

At this stage, it is not yet clear whether these represent:

1. One business concept with multiple implementations.
2. Multiple business concepts that should remain separate.

---

## Decision

No architectural changes will be made at this time.

The Student Achievements feature is **temporarily postponed** until the product rules are clarified.

No repositories, entities, Firestore schemas, blocs, or feature ownership will be modified before a
product decision is made.

---

## Reason

Implementing the feature now would require making assumptions about the product requirements.

The project rule is:

> Never redesign the architecture based on assumptions.

Instead, architectural changes must follow explicit product decisions.

---

## Pending Product Questions

The following questions must be answered before implementation begins:

### 1. Relationship

Are "Achievements" and "Awards" the same business concept?

Or are they two different concepts?

---

### 2. Badges

Are badges part of the Gamification system?

Or are they teacher-granted awards?

---

### 3. Supervisor

Should supervisor-issued achievements use the same workflow as teacher awards?

Or are they independent?

---

### 4. Firestore Schema

Should the project standardize on a single document schema?

Or intentionally support multiple schemas?

---

## Next Action

Pause implementation of the Student Achievements feature.

Continue development with other student features that do not require unresolved product decisions.

Once the product rules are clarified, reopen this ADR and implement the approved architecture.
